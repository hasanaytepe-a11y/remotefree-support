Support

Need help with RemoteFree for Samsung TV? Try the steps below or contact us.

Getting connected

  • Make sure your iPhone or iPad and TV are connected to the same local Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Local Network access when iOS asks for permission.
  • Select your TV in the app and accept the connection request shown on the TV.

If your TV is not found

  • Confirm that the TV is powered on and connected to Wi-Fi.
  • Reopen the app and try scanning again.
  • Check that Local Network access is enabled for the app in iOS Settings.
  • Restart the TV, phone, or Wi-Fi router if the issue continues.

Cost and account

The app is free to use, has no subscription, and does not require a Samsung account.
