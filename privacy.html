Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RemoteFree for Samsung TV handles information.

Local network communication

The app uses your local Wi-Fi network to discover and communicate with compatible TVs. Remote-control commands and pairing communication are exchanged locally between your device and your TV.

Pairing information

Pairing credentials or tokens needed to reconnect to a TV may be stored securely on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's remote-control functionality.

Personal data

Based on the app's current functionality, the app does not intentionally collect, transmit to the developer, sell, or share personal data. No Samsung account is required.

Advertising and analytics

The current policy does not describe any advertising or analytics service. If a future version adds an advertising SDK, analytics, crash reporting, or another third-party service that processes data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before that version is released.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
